ClueStar — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

ClueStar ("ClueStar", "we", "our", or "us") is a voice-powered educational guessing game designed for children.

We respect the privacy of children and families. This Privacy Policy explains what information ClueStar accesses, how it is used, and how it is handled when you use the app.

2. Information We Access

Microphone and Voice Input

ClueStar uses the device microphone when a player chooses to answer a question by speaking.

Spoken answers are processed through the online speech-recognition functionality used by the app to convert speech into text and determine whether the spoken answer matches the expected answer.

ClueStar requires an internet connection for voice-answer recognition. Voice recognition is not performed solely on the device.

ClueStar does not use the microphone for continuous background recording. The microphone is activated when the player chooses to provide a spoken answer.

3. Internet Connection

ClueStar requires an internet connection for voice-answer recognition. The app checks for an internet connection before voice recognition is used.

4. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

ClueStar does not intentionally request or collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Home addresses
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Payment information
  • Passwords
  • User account or login credentials

5. Children's Privacy

ClueStar is designed with children and families in mind. The app does not intentionally request personal information such as a child's name, email address, phone number, home address, or precise location.

ClueStar does not knowingly sell children's personal information. We aim to comply with applicable children's privacy requirements, including relevant Google Play Families policies and applicable laws.

6. Advertising

ClueStar currently does not display advertisements. If advertising is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy and the relevant app-store disclosures will be updated.

7. Analytics and Tracking

ClueStar currently does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising profiling or behavioral advertising. If analytics or other third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

8. Third-Party Services

ClueStar may rely on third-party technology and platform services necessary for speech recognition and app operation. These services may process information necessary to provide their functionality. Their processing is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

ClueStar does not provide user accounts or maintain a developer-operated personal profile database. The app does not intentionally retain children's personal information.

Voice input is used for speech recognition to determine the player's answer. Any retention or handling by a third-party speech-recognition provider is governed by that provider's applicable policies.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled through ClueStar. Third-party services used by the app are subject to their own security and privacy practices.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, services, or applicable legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published at the same privacy-policy location with a revised effective date.
