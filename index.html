ClueStar — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

ClueStar ("ClueStar", "we", "our", or "us") is a voice-powered educational guessing game designed for children.

We respect the privacy of children and families. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed through ClueStar, how it is used, and how it is handled when you use the app.

2. Microphone and Voice Input

ClueStar uses the device microphone when a player chooses to answer a question by speaking.

Spoken answers are processed through speech-recognition services available on the device to convert speech into text and determine whether the spoken answer matches the expected answer.

Speech recognition may involve processing by third-party speech-recognition providers. Audio and related information may be transmitted to those providers, depending on the device and recognition service being used.

ClueStar does not use the microphone for continuous background recording. The microphone is activated when the player chooses to provide a spoken answer.

3. Internet Connection

ClueStar requires an internet connection for certain features, including speech recognition and advertisements. These services may involve communication with third-party service providers.

4. Information Processed Through Third-Party Services

ClueStar does not directly ask players to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, home address, contacts, or payment information.

However, third-party services integrated into ClueStar, including advertising and speech-recognition services, may process certain information, such as:

  • Approximate location information derived from an IP address
  • App interactions and usage information
  • Device identifiers
  • Diagnostics and performance information
  • Voice input and speech-recognition information, when a player chooses to answer using the microphone

The information processed depends on the service, device, settings, and functionality being used.

5. Children's Privacy

ClueStar is designed for children and families. We aim to provide an age-appropriate experience and use advertising and services in accordance with applicable Google Play Families requirements and applicable laws.

ClueStar does not intentionally ask children to provide their names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or precise location.

We do not knowingly sell children's personal information.

6. Advertising

ClueStar uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob and related advertising services may process certain information, including device identifiers, IP address-derived approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostics, for purposes related to advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation, as applicable.

ClueStar is designed for children and uses child-directed advertising treatment and restrictions on advertising content.

We do not intentionally use ClueStar to create personalized advertising profiles of children.

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising service providers in accordance with their applicable privacy policies and practices.

7. Analytics and Diagnostics

Third-party services integrated into ClueStar may process certain app interactions, device information, and diagnostics to support app functionality, performance, stability, advertising measurement, and related service operation, as applicable.

ClueStar does not intentionally use these services to create personal profiles of children for behavioral advertising.

8. Third-Party Services

ClueStar relies on third-party services necessary for certain app functionality, including speech-recognition services and Google AdMob.

These services may process information such as device information, technical information, app interactions, voice input, or information required to provide their functionality.

The processing of information by third-party providers is governed by their respective privacy policies, terms, and applicable practices.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. Data Retention and Deletion

ClueStar does not provide user accounts or maintain a developer-operated personal profile database.

Voice input is used for speech recognition to determine the player's answer. ClueStar does not intentionally maintain a database of children's voice recordings or personal profiles.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained or deleted according to the applicable provider's retention, deletion, and privacy practices.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through ClueStar. Third-party services used by the app are subject to their own security and privacy practices.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, services, or applicable legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published at the same privacy-policy location with a revised effective date.
